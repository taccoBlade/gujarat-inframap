Architecture, Geometry & Precision

Built with Leaflet.js v1.9 and optimized with GDAL/OGR coordinate rounding to 5 decimal places (~1.1 meter ground precision). Layer opacities are hardware-composited on custom CSS panes for 60 FPS performance without vector re-rendering. Desktop GIS integration provided via preconfigured gujarat_inframap.qgs for QGIS 4.0.1.

Seasonal Hydrology & Peaking Energy Dynamics

MONSOON (JUL–OCT)

Heavy catchment inflows fill reservoir to Full Reservoir Level (FRL). High gross head and surplus spillway surcharges produce peak firm and secondary energy.

WINTER RABI (NOV–FEB)

Irrigation releases into canal networks generate electricity at Canal Head Power Houses (CHPH) and dam toes, synchronizing food security with clean power.

SUMMER LEAN (MAR–JUN)

Reservoir stage approaches MDDL. Hydro generation is strictly conserved for high-value peak shaving (1–3 hours/day) and grid frequency defense.
