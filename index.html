EDITORIAL MISSION & DATA TRANSPARENCY

About Gujarat InfraMap & Data Methods

A public-service infrastructure explorer built to help Gujarat residents, students, researchers, travellers, and journalists discover, understand, and explore the state's physical infrastructure.

Purpose & Scope

Gujarat InfraMap is an exploratory public information platform. It is not an engineering design CAD, an AI prediction engine, or a live SCADA operational twin. Its purpose is to answer everyday questions: Where is this dam or station located? What does it do? Who operates it? What infrastructure exists in my district?

By translating complex spatial data into an accessible, responsive cartographic experience, it bridges the gap between statutory government gazettes and citizen awareness.

Data Credibility & Feature Count Audit

In earlier exploratory prototypes, a figure of "7,344 features" was displayed. A strict spatial audit reconciled that count: the repository contains exactly 3,337 verified features across 13 layer datasets.

The difference reflects data consolidation: the original OpenStreetMap road dataset contained 4,050 raw road segments, which our automated ETL pipeline merged and deduplicated into 43 continuous, unified highway corridors (e.g. NH 48, NE-1) for cartographic clarity.

Coordinate Resolution vs Source Accuracy

Coordinates in Gujarat InfraMap are represented to five decimal places (e.g., 22.30942° N, 72.13621° E), which provides roughly metre-scale numerical precision in software calculations.

However, numerical precision does not imply centimetre survey accuracy. The positional accuracy of underlying features varies by origin:

  • Railway Stations & Power Plants: 10–25 metre positional accuracy (verified against high-resolution satellite imagery and official survey benchmarks).
  • CWC Major Dams & Reservoirs: 15–50 metre positional accuracy of crest structures and water-spread polygons.
  • Highway Corridors: Alignment accuracy consistent with 1:50,000 national mapping scales and OpenStreetMap GPS-trace compilation.
  • District & Administrative Boundaries: Normalized against Survey of India revenue boundaries at state scale.

Provenance, Licensing & Downloads

All spatial data processed by Gujarat InfraMap is sourced from publicly accessible statutory government records, published gazettes, and open geographic datasets:

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM): Licensed under Open Database License (ODbL). © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Central Water Commission (CWC NRLD): Public records published under the National Register of Large Dams.
  • Central Electricity Authority (CEA): Monthly generation reports & PowerGrid transmission maps.
  • National Data & Analytics Platform (NDAP) / Data.gov.in: Open Government Data (OGD) License India.
