# Gujarat InfraMap — Distinctive Gujarati Cultural Design System

## Visual Identity: Contemporary Gujarati Neo-Brutalism

This design system draws directly from the living craft traditions, architectural wonders, and indigenous visual cultures of Gujarat:
- **Handmade Kagdi Paper**: Warm off-white ivory canvas (`#FDFBF7`, `#F8F4EC`) reminiscent of the historic 15th-century papermakers of Ahmedabad.
- **Patan Indigo & Madder Red**: Natural resist-dye palette (`#1A2E40` and `#9B2226`) derived from iron, pomegranate, and indigo vats.
- **Turmeric Gold, Neem Green & Dholavira Sandstone**: Natural earth pigments (`#DDA15E`, `#2A9D8F`, `#D4A373`).
- **Ink-Black Outlines & Controlled Hard Shadows**: Neo-brutalist structural discipline (`border-2 border-[#18181B]` with `shadow-[3px_3px_0px_#18181B]`).

## 7 Authentic Gujarati SVG Design Assets

### 1. Patola Double-Ikat Geometry (`PatolaIkatPattern`)
- **Cultural Source**: Patan Patola Silk Weavers (Salvi community, North Gujarat), particularly the classic *Chhabdi Bhat* (Basket pattern) and *Navratna Bhat* (Nine Jewels).
- **Intended Role**: Highlighting mathematical precision, passport gold stamps, primary active navigation indicators, and monumental heritage cards (e.g. Rani ki Vav).
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Strictly secular mathematical geometry; used for architectural and cultural excellence markers.

### 2. Ajrakh Geometric Star Lattice (`AjrakhStarPattern`)
- **Cultural Source**: Khatri artisans of Dhamalka and Ajrakhpur (Kachchh), dating back to the Indus Valley civilization's block-printing techniques.
- **Intended Role**: 8-pointed star rosette used as identity seals, modal headers, fact-check verified pins, and district explorer challenge emblems.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Respects the geometric Islamic and Vedic symmetry of desert dye craft without modifying the sacred 8-point matrix.

### 3. Bandhani Dot Clusters (`BandhaniDotsPattern`)
- **Cultural Source**: Jamnagar and Bhuj tied-resist dyeing (*Bandhej*), where skilled fingers knot fabric with cotton thread to create micro-droplets (*bundi*).
- **Intended Role**: Scatter clusters, milestone markers, category indicator dots, and subtle borders.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Festive, vibrant, and welcoming. Avoid placing over dense geographic map data.

### 4. Stepwell Descending Geometry (`StepwellGeometryPattern`)
- **Cultural Source**: Maru-Gurjara subterranean water architecture (Rani ki Vav, Patan; Adalaj Vav, Gandhinagar; Dada Harir, Ahmedabad).
- **Intended Role**: Structural dividers, progress gauges, chevron corner brackets, and water-infrastructure asset dossiers.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Used as abstract architectural framing rather than generic photo wallpaper.

### 5. Toran Auspicious Border Trim (`ToranOrnamentalBorder`)
- **Cultural Source**: Auspicious threshold hangings (*Toran*) crafted with *Asopalav* (Polyalthia longifolia) mango leaves, marigold floral balls (*Genda phool*), and Saurashtra mirror embroidery (*Abhla / Lippan*).
- **Intended Role**: Top header trim, card top ribbons, and modal threshold banners.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Represents hospitable welcome (*Padharo Mhare Des / પધારો*); kept clean and unobtrusive.

### 6. Pol Carved Teakwood Framing (`PolWoodCarvingPattern`)
- **Cultural Source**: UNESCO World Heritage Old Ahmedabad Pol architecture (15th–18th century carved brackets / *Todla*, wooden struts with peacocks, and floral geometric jalis).
- **Intended Role**: Image frame corner brackets, editorial article frames, and drawer handles.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Expresses civic Gujarati merchant heritage and structural craftsmanship.

### 7. Pithora Sacred Tribal Motif (`PithoraEditorialMotif`)
- **Cultural Source**: Rathwa and Bhil indigenous sacred mural paintings (*Lakhara* tradition) from the Chhota Udepur and Panchmahal forest belt of Eastern Gujarat.
- **Intended Role**: Editorial watermark, nature & forest reserve dossiers, indigenous story cards, and Eastern Gujarat tribal belt cultural attribution.
- **Contextual Guidelines**: Sacred ritual tradition. Rendered with dignity, restraint, and attribution as an illustrative silhouette rather than casual decoration.

## Fluid Transition & Interactive Architecture
- **Main Landing Page**: The interactive **Atlas Map** (`currentMode = 'explore'`) is the primary digital twin interface.
- **Fluid Cross-Page Animation**: Smooth page enter/exit motions powered by Framer Motion with architectural cubic-bezier timing (`[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`).
- **Strict Visual Hierarchy**: The interactive map remains 100% unobstructed by decorative textures; SVG patterns are confined to chrome, cards, drawers, and modal headers.
